The Silk Legacy

A Caravan of Shared Dreams

The AJ Wings story began over a century ago in the shadow of the Umayyad Mosque. Our founders, generations of explorers and merchants, understood that travel was never just about the destination—it was about the bridge built between hearts.

Leaving the jasmine-scented streets of Syria was not an end, but an expansion. We brought the philosophy of the Majlis—the warm, hospitable gathering—to the world of modern luxury travel.

"We do not just show you the world; we invite you into its soul, treating every traveler like a guest in our own home."
